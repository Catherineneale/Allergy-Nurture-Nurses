## Sample brand copy (from the branding PDF)

> Allergies, asthma, and infant feeding concerns can feel overwhelming for parents. We take the time to listen to your child's health history properly, providing calm, clear, and practical self-management guidance to help your family move forward with absolute confidence.

## Voice and tone

Calm, warm, reassuring, plain English, professional but not clinical-cold. Short paragraphs. Repeats key search/positioning terms naturally (baby allergy, Non-IgE, CMPA, allergic rhinitis, eczema). Never alarmist, but includes clear safety-netting/escalation language where relevant (999 / NHS 111 / GP).

## Photography style (from headshots shared, not saved as files)
