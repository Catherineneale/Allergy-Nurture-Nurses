What Allergy Nurture Nurses does and does not provide

Allergy Nurture Nurses is a CQC-registered, nurse-led allergy and asthma advisory service for children, young people and families.

We provide education, support and practical guidance. We do not provide emergency medical care, allergy testing, medical diagnosis or prescribing. Where appropriate we can liaise with the patient's GP and signpost to appropriate services. Where further medical assessment is required, families will be advised to contact their GP, NHS services or an appropriate healthcare professional. In a medical emergency, call 999.

Your baby or child should remain under their GP, NHS team or relevant healthcare professionals for clinical diagnosis, medical treatment, prescribing and urgent care.

When should I seek urgent medical help?

Most Eczema symptoms are not emergencies. However, allergic reactions can occasionally become serious very quickly.

Call 999 immediately if your baby or child develops difficulty breathing, significant swelling of the tongue or throat, becomes floppy, collapses, appears severely unwell or develops symptoms of anaphylaxis.

Urgent medical advice should also be sought if your baby's skin appears significantly infected or your baby is becoming unwell. Urgent symptoms are not suitable for a routine consultation or online enquiry.
