I wanted to introduce Allergy Nurture Nurses, a CQC-registered, nurse-led remote allergy and asthma advisory service supporting children and families across the UK — particularly around Non-IgE food allergy, eczema, allergic rhinitis and allergy-related respiratory/asthma concerns.

Allergy Nurture Nurses gives families dedicated time with a specialist nurse to understand their child's symptoms, receive clear, evidence-based advice, and know when to seek further medical review — working alongside their GP and existing NHS care, not replacing it.

I'm reaching out to ask whether you'd be happy to send our attached A5 flyer home in children's book bags, so that families in your setting who may be navigating allergy-related concerns know the service is available to them.
