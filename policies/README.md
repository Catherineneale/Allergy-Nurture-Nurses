## Purpose snapshots (one line each, from the "Purpose/Policy Statement" section of each document — see the docx itself for full detail)

- **Consent (3):** consent must be valid, informed, freely given and documented; children's rights, voices and autonomy are respected; incorporates Mental Capacity Act 2005 and Gillick competency.
- **Duty of Candour & Incident Reporting (5):** culture of honesty, openness, learning and patient partnership; complies with the statutory Duty of Candour (Health & Social Care Act 2008, Regulation 20).
- **Complaints (6):** complaints welcomed as opportunities for learning and improvement, and recognised as a patient's right under law.
- **Cyber Security & Digital Safety (8):** protects children/family personal information and patient health information in a secure digital environment.
- **Clinical Supervision, Reflective Practice & CPD (12):** ensures clinical practice is safe and evidence-based through supervision and ongoing development.
- **Child, Parent & Family Communication & Accessibility (14):** clear, compassionate, patient/child-centred, family-inclusive, accessible, equitable, culturally respectful, neuro-affirming and trauma-informed communication.
- **Clinical Documentation & Record Keeping (17):** records are accurate, clear, contemporaneous, secure, confidential, safeguarding-compliant, evidence-based and legally defensible.
- **Clinical Risk Assessment & Escalation (18):** early identification, assessment and escalation of clinical risk to protect patients, families, clinicians and service integrity; safety-first, anticipatory approach.
- **Business Continuity & Emergency Data Recovery (22):** maintains essential services during disruption, protects families from harm due to care interruption, secure clinical record storage (Semble), rapid system restoration after an outage/cyber event.
- **Clinical Audit & Quality Improvement (23):** high-quality, safe, effective allergy care through systematic audit, continuous quality improvement, evidence-based practice, and accountability.
