# Allergy Nurture Nurses — Introduction Video Script (90-second version)

**Estimated length:** ~85–100 seconds depending on pace (roughly 227 words). See `introduction-video-script.md` for the ~60 second version.

---

Hi everyone, and welcome.

Allergy Nurture Nurses is a nurse-led, Care Quality Commission regulated service, providing evidence-based advice to help children, young people and families understand and manage allergic conditions.

Allergic conditions can feel complex and confusing, so Allergy Nurture Nurses takes time to listen, using a detailed history to build a clear picture of each family's health, and offering practical guidance to improve everyday quality of life.

The service specialises in delayed food allergy, often called Non-IgE food allergy, including cow's milk allergy, alongside eczema, allergic rhinitis or hay fever, other environmental triggers such as dust mite and mould, and allergy-triggered asthma.

Allergy Nurture Nurses is led by a Registered Children's Nurse with 25 years' experience, the last seven specialising in allergy, caring for children and young people from birth to nineteen.

Allergy Nurture Nurses is passionate about giving every child the best start in life, and supporting the whole family, because person-centred, holistic care matters most when conditions are so often intertwined.

Families often say the same thing: not enough time to be properly heard, and services that are hard to access, long waiting times, travel, and time off work or school.

That's why Allergy Nurture Nurses is delivered remotely, making support accessible to families right across the UK.

Every child deserves the best start in life. Thank you for listening, we look forward to helping you.
