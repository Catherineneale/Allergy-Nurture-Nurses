# Allergy Nurture Nurses — Introduction Video Script

**Estimated length:** ~60–65 seconds at a natural, unhurried narration pace (roughly 145 words). Trim the bracketed [optional] line if you need to land strictly under 60 seconds. See `introduction-video-script-90s.md` for a fuller 90-second version.

---

Hi everyone, and welcome.

Allergy Nurture Nurses is a nurse-led, Care Quality Commission regulated service, providing evidence-based advice to help children, young people and families understand and manage allergic conditions.

Allergy Nurture Nurses takes the time to listen, using a detailed history to build a clear picture of each family's health, and offering practical guidance to improve everyday quality of life.

The service specialises in delayed food allergy, often called Non-IgE allergy, including cow's milk allergy, alongside eczema, allergic rhinitis, environmental triggers such as dust mite and mould, and allergy-triggered asthma.

[Led by a Registered Children's Nurse with 25 years' experience, the last seven in allergy, Allergy Nurture Nurses is passionate about giving every child the best start in life, and supporting the whole family.]

Families often say the same thing: not enough time to be heard, and services that are hard to reach. That's why Allergy Nurture Nurses is delivered remotely, accessible to families right across the UK.

Every child deserves the best start in life. Thank you for listening, we look forward to helping you.

---
